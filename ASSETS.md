## New asset prompts

### Sky — dist/assets/sky.webp
Ultra-wide cinematic atmospheric background photograph for a premium airline website hero. A view high above a sea of dark clouds at blue hour, warm champagne horizon on the right, deep nearly black teal sky, silver and ivory cloud edges. Left and upper area kept clean for typography. No aircraft, text, logos or UI.

### Business — dist/assets/business.webp
Photorealistic premium airline business class editorial photograph. Ivory lie-flat seat, forest-green privacy shell, champagne details, wood console, reading lamp, oval windows with sunlit clouds. Authentic modern widebody open aisle-facing pod. No people, logos, text or UI.

### Economy — dist/assets/economy.webp
Photorealistic premium airline economy cabin. Three upright forest-green seats, cream headrest covers and champagne piping, oval windows with daylight and clouds, warm ceiling lights, authentic neat cabin proportions. No people, text, logos or UI.
